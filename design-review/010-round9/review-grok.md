## What works

- The permission card is a real component: orange stroke, command in a well, a plain-language reason, a note field, and one filled Allow.
- The activity row is a system worth keeping: status symbol, title, “Needs you · 2m ago”, chevron. It reads the same on the dashboard and on iPad.
- Agent discs are identity, and on the phone lists that color stays inside the circle instead of tinting the name.
- Settings and the article are paced like system apps: grouped rows, a calm destructive button, a real title and byline, comfortable line length.
- The compact Live Activity is the right mark for this product: the indigo glyph, a three-color ring, and a single count.

## To reach 9+

1. Lock the color contract in problem 2, and apply it to flags, overdue dates, agent rows, widgets, and the Live Activity together. That is the fastest lift for Color and Consistency.
2. Reflow every iPad detail pane and the extra-large widget for the width they have (problem 1), and quiet the sidebar selection (problem 10). That lifts Spacing, Native, and Hierarchy.
3. Make chat and actor sessions one reading column: clear the composer and the nav bar, scroll code and tables sideways, and use a single date format (problems 4, 5, 8, 11). That lifts Consistency and Typography.
4. Lead Dashboard and Intelligence with what needs you, and demote the greeting and the pinned portraits (problem 3). The eye should land on a decision.
5. Rebuild the widgets so each size fills, the discs match the app, and the sentence matches the rows (problems 6 and 7). A professional judges a tool by the surfaces that sit on the Home Screen.
