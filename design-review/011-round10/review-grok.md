## What works

- The tab bar and the circular toolbar buttons are the right Liquid Glass chrome, and the selected tab pill is clear.
- The orange permission card in `06-iphone-actor-session-light` and `13d-ipad-actor-session-light` is the one custom component with a real primary action: filled Allow, quiet Deny, command set apart.
- What’s New already has the right status grammar when the icon and the word travel together: orange hand “Needs you,” red xmark “Failed,” check “Done.”
- The article in `34-article-light` has a real title, byline, measure, and code well. It is the type reference for chat and documents.
- The compact Live Activity ring in `59-live-activity-compact-light` says “1 needs you” in a single glance, and the lock-screen rows are the right density for that surface.

## To reach 9+

1. **Stop the chrome collisions and lock one navigation rule.** Inset every scrolling screen above the tab bar and below the nav bar, and use one title and toolbar pattern for root, push, and sheet. That is the fastest lift for Native, Spacing, and Consistency.
2. **Put the urgent object first.** Dashboard shows Working above the fold, Intelligence opens on Needs you, Workspace opens on Today. One primary action per screen. That moves Hierarchy more than any visual polish.
3. **One type ramp and one date style, and nothing clips.** `.subheadline` secondary for metadata, semibold for unread and section labels, monospaced scroll for code, a 640pt measure on iPad. Typography and polish both depend on this.
4. **Split identity color from status color, and use one symbol style.** Agent hues leave orange, red, and green. Status is a labeled symbol. List icons are secondary symbols on gray squares, including Settings and Extensions. That repairs Color and Iconography together.
5. **Design the wide sizes.** iPad tasks get grouped sections and aligned columns. Agent and task widgets reflow per size instead of leaving a blank field, and the count on the widget matches the rows. A professional judges the iPad and the home screen as the product, not as a stretched phone.
