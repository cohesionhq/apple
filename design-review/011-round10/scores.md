## What changed in round 10, and why

Round 10 takes the maintainer's answers on the forks the reviewers kept
raising, and matches the web (`../cohesion`) where it decides:

- **High priority** is a yellow-orange flag, clear of the amber that says
  "needs you"; urgent stays red. The widgets share it.
- **Greeting**: iOS greets with the profile's name as the web does; the
  preview profile now holds the first name, as the maintainer's does.
- **One title rule**: every tab's first screen has a large title (the
  Dashboard's greeting stands in); every pushed screen's is inline. Settings
  moves from the leading edge to the trailing edge with each tab's buttons.
  Workspace's and Feeds' large titles are menus (`titleMenu`): the name and
  a chevron at the bar's leading edge, since the system's large title draws
  no chevron for a title menu.
- **Feeds opens on the articles**, as the web's Articles page does; the feed
  is picked, or one added, from the title's menu.
- **iPad tasks**: a row ends in columns, as the web's do (the due day, the
  assignee, the flag), in a wider column (960pt).

After the shots, committed with them: the flag's column keeps its width on a
row with no flag, so the columns before it hold still (Opus saw them shift);
and the Agents widget's "need you" is amber at every size.
