## Scores

| Category | Score | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Native | 7.7 | Glass toolbars, floating tab bar, inset-grouped lists, sheets and the iPad sidebar are right, but pinned titles and composers sit on top of content with no scroll-edge effect, and the Dashboard composer is a shadowed card, not glass. |
| Typography | 7.3 | The article reader and list rows are well set, but there are two section-header styles, very small tertiary metadata (tool rows, "Noted:" lines, chips) and inconsistent date formats. |
| Hierarchy | 6.9 | The Dashboard spends its first third on a two-line greeting; Due/What's New headers are grey and weak; the iPad Dashboard leaves 40% blank; "Working" rows look disabled. |
| Spacing & density | 6.8 | Unexplained ~40pt bands under the toolbars, loose Dashboard gaps, separator insets that change within one card, half-empty widgets. |
| Color & contrast | 6.4 | Orange, red, green and indigo are reused for priority flags, size chips, "Not Fetched" and Settings tiles, so the semantic colours stop meaning one thing. |
| Iconography | 6.9 | Outline-indigo SF Symbols, Settings-style filled tiles, grey letter monograms and emoji avatars all compete; the Dashboard tab glyph is unclear; the Extensions tile glyph is cropped. |
| Consistency & polish | 6.5 | Three different composers, two title placements, the flag colour changes between list and detail, "Yesterday" vs "yesterday", and the widgets disagree on the count. |
| Overall | 7.0 | A capable, mostly native app with a few really designed moments (reader, permission card, iPad tasks), but the rules aren't applied evenly enough to read as one designer's work. |

## Problems

1. **13b-ipad-agent-chat, 13d-ipad-actor-session, 05-iphone-agent-chat:** content runs straight under the floating chrome. In 13b the "Atlas >" pill sits on top of "wins on color"; in 13d the title "Add backoff to the change socket's reconnect / apple" is printed over the diff; in 05 the table rows ("2026-11-12 You") show through the "Message Atlas" field, and a reply line shows *below* the composer. **Fix:** apply the standard soft scroll-edge effect at top and bottom (`.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .all)`). Inset the scroll content by the composer's height (`safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`) so the last message stops above the bar. On iPad, use a real inline navigation title for the agent or session, not a centred floating pill.

2. **07-iphone-tasks, 13c-ipad-tasks, 54/55/56 widgets, 26-task-detail:** the priority flags use indigo, orange, red and grey. Indigo is the brand accent, orange means "needs you" and red means failed, so a Medium flag in indigo and a High flag in orange take over meanings the app already owns. **Fix:** show priority with one hue at different strengths: red filled flag for urgent, `.secondary` filled for high, outlined `.tertiary` for medium, no flag for low/none (or Reminders-style `!!!` in `.secondary`). Never use indigo or orange for priority.

3. **07-iphone-tasks, 13c-ipad-tasks:** the size chips are a rainbow (Tiny cyan, Small green, Medium blue, Large pink, Huge red). Green ("connected") and red ("failed") are reused for task size, and five extra hues make the list loud. **Fix:** draw size as one neutral chip (`.quaternary` fill, `.secondary` text) with a graded glyph (one to five bars), or a plain-text "S / M / L" token. Keep colour for state only.

4. **01-iphone-dashboard:** the greeting wraps to two lines at a very large size. With the date and the loose gaps, the first ~640pt hold no actionable content, and only four Due rows fit above the tab bar. **Fix:** use a one-line large title ("Today" or "Monday, Nov 9") with the greeting as a `.subheadline` `.secondary` line, or shorten the greeting to `.title` weight. Cut the date→composer gap from ~38pt to 16pt and the composer→Due gap from ~50pt to 24pt.

5. **01-iphone-dashboard, 05-iphone-agent-chat, 06-iphone-actor-session, 13d:** there are three different composers. The Dashboard has a white card with a drop shadow, a placeholder on top and a toolbar row of agent picker, image and send. Chat has a single glass capsule with the image button inside. The actor session has a separate glass circle (spinner), a capsule field and a separate red stop circle. **Fix:** define one composer component (glass capsule, leading attach button inside, trailing send/stop that morphs) and use it everywhere. On the Dashboard, consider moving it into the tab bar's bottom accessory (`tabViewBottomAccessory`) as a glass "Ask Atlas…" bar. That is native Liquid Glass, reachable with the thumb, and removes the duplicate compose button in the toolbar.

6. **01-iphone-dashboard, 13-ipad-split-view:** "Due 4" and "What's New 4" are large semibold headers in grey with a lighter grey count. They are bigger than grouped-list headers ("Agents", "Tasks" in 02/03) but lower in contrast, so the page has two header systems and its main sections look disabled. **Fix:** use `.title3.weight(.semibold)` in `.primary` with the count in `.secondary`, or the standard grouped-list header style if the Dashboard moves to inset-grouped. Pick one header style for the whole app.

7. **01-iphone-dashboard, 13-ipad-split-view:** the Dashboard is a plain white list while every other top-level screen (02, 03, 04, 07, 08, 10) is inset-grouped on grey. Its first impression doesn't match the rest of the app. **Fix:** make the Dashboard inset-grouped like its siblings: Due in one card, What's New in one card, the composer above on the grouped background. Or move the other screens to the plain style. Don't mix them.

8. **13-ipad-split-view:** in the two-column Dashboard the bottom ~45% of both columns is empty, the composer is a small strip, the toolbar has only a compose button, and the "Working" row ("Polish the iPad sidebar spacing") has a grey title that looks disabled. **Fix:** give the columns content that fills the space: Due plus "Up next" (tomorrow) on the left; What's New plus Working plus Actors on the right; or a third card with today's journal entry. Keep the "Working" title in `.primary` and let the indigo "Working" status carry the state.

9. **07-iphone-tasks (vs 08, 10, 44), 13c:** titles are placed differently on similar pushed screens. "Inbox" is left-aligned next to the back button; "Journal", "Documents" and "Extensions" are centred inline; "All ⌄" on iPad is a small centred menu title. **Fix:** pushed list screens should use a standard large title that collapses to a centred inline title on scroll. Inbox should match Journal and Documents.

10. **03, 04, 07, 08, 44 (vs 10, 14):** an empty ~40pt band sits between the toolbar and the first card. It reads as a large title that failed to render. Documents and Settings start right under the bar, so the rhythm changes from screen to screen. **Fix:** remove the extra top inset, or fill it with the large title (fix 9). Content should start one standard section gap (~20pt) below the bar.

11. **26-task-detail vs 07/13c:** "Book flights for the Lisbon offsite" has an indigo flag in the list and on iPad, but the detail's Priority row shows a grey flag for "Medium". **Fix:** the picker's leading flag must use the same priority presentation as the rows (from `ProtocolPresentation`), after fix 2 is applied.

12. **26-task-detail:** the form mixes several kinds of control. "Set Size" is an indigo button while siblings show grey values ("Incomplete", "Inbox", "None"). Tags uses a disclosure chevron while the others use pop-up chevrons. Notes are a read-only line plus an "Edit Notes >" row. The confirm checkmark in the top right is a grey glass circle that looks disabled. **Fix:** show Size as a pop-up menu with "None" in `.secondary` like the others. Make the notes an inline multi-line `TextField(axis: .vertical)` in the card. Make the title field the visual lead (no separate card, `.title3` semibold). Style the confirm button `.glassProminent` with the accent when there are changes, and dim it only when there are none.

13. **04-iphone-feeds:** "Not Fetched" is in orange. Orange means "needs you"; a fetch failure is either red (failed) or neutral. The meta line also runs author and feed together with a double space ("Ines Kowalski  Swift Field Notes"). **Fix:** set "Not fetched" in `.secondary` with a small `exclamationmark.circle` glyph (or red if it really failed). Join the meta with " · ". Use sentence case for status text.

14. **04-iphone-feeds vs 10-documents vs 01/13:** time formats disagree. Feeds says "yesterday" in lowercase, Documents "Yesterday"; Dashboard "2m ago", Feeds "2 hours ago", Documents "40 minutes ago"; the chat table "2026-11-12", tasks "Nov 12". **Fix:** use one relative formatter everywhere: `.relative(presentation: .named, unitsStyle: .abbreviated)` for lists and `Date.FormatStyle` with `.month(.abbreviated).day()` for dates. Capitalise at the start of a line. Render ISO dates in agent tables as "Nov 12".

15. **50-widget-agents-small vs 51/52:** the same three avatars give "3 need you" on the small widget and "2 — Milo and Juno need you." on the medium and large (Ada is "Reading 12 of 40 sources"). The INDEX says the small shows unread messages, but the label says "need you". **Fix:** if the small counts unread, label it "unread" in `.secondary`. If it counts needs-you, show only the two orange agents. One widget family should never disagree with itself.

16. **51-widget-agents-medium, 52-widget-agents-large:** in the medium, three large avatars fill the left half, the right half is empty, and the number repeats the sentence ("2 Milo and Juno need you."). The large shows the same three agents twice: giant avatars, then a list. **Fix (medium):** a 2×2 layout with the needs-you count and names on the left (`.title` number, `.subheadline` orange names) and up to three agent rows on the right, each with avatar, name and status. **Fix (large):** drop the oversized avatar stack, keep a header line ("2 need you"), and list up to six agents with their status. Remove the trailing period in a widget label.

17. **53/54-widget-tasks-small/medium:** both show only two tasks with "7 more" and leave the bottom ~30% empty. The small truncates "Review the ro…". **Fix:** fill the height. The small takes three rows at `.subheadline`, the medium four rows at `.subheadline` with dates trailing. Put "7 more" in the header next to the count ("Today · 9") instead of on its own line.

18. **55 vs 54/56 widgets:** the large tasks widget puts dates under titles, while medium and extra-large put them trailing. The extra-large is one narrow list spread across a wide canvas with ~60% blank in the middle. **Fix:** use trailing dates in every size. Make the extra-large two columns (Overdue | Today, or two task views), or add the folder and assignee columns from 13c.

19. **57/58 Live Activity:** the summary glyphs (orange hand, dotted indigo ring, grey check) are tiny next to their numbers. "+3 more" duplicates what the counts already say. The timers ("0:40", "5:13") are unlabelled. The segmented bar's order (done, working, needs-you) puts the most urgent segment last. **Fix:** drop "+3 more" (the counts cover it), put the needs-you segment first, and label elapsed time with a `timer` glyph or "0:40 elapsed" in `.caption` `.secondary`.

20. **59-live-activity-compact:** the compact leading slot spends itself on the app logo, and the trailing ring holds an orange "1" too small to read. **Fix:** leading shows the orange hand glyph with "1" at `.caption.bold`; trailing shows the ring alone, or "2" working in indigo. The logo belongs only in the minimal and expanded views.

21. **14-settings, 17-agent-info vs 03-workspace, 13 sidebar:** there are two icon languages. Settings and agent info use colourful filled squircle tiles (orange Profile, red Notifications, green Live Activities), while Workspace and the sidebar use indigo outline symbols. The tile colours also reuse the semantic orange, red and green. The Extensions tile glyph looks cropped, and the Clients glyph is visibly smaller than its neighbours. **Fix:** tiles are acceptable in Settings because iOS does it, but draw them from a restrained set (indigo, grey, plus the system's own red only for Notifications) and size glyphs optically (`.imageScale(.medium)` in a 29pt tile). Use `puzzlepiece.extension.fill`, not a custom shape.

22. **44-extensions:** every extension is a grey letter tile (G, L, D, I, C). They look like placeholders, and nothing shows enabled, disabled or update state. **Fix:** use the extension's own icon when the daemon supplies one. Otherwise use a meaningful SF Symbol on an indigo-tinted tile (`arrow.triangle.branch` for GitHub, `checkmark.seal` for Done Stamp), and add a trailing state ("On", "Update") in `.secondary`.

23. **02-iphone-intelligence, 13 sidebar:** Scout's name is greyed in both places while its avatar stays bright green, which is a mixed signal with no explanation. The pinned avatars are 120pt, far larger than anything else, and leave the row two-thirds empty. **Fix:** show offline state explicitly (a grey status dot on the avatar, plus "Offline" in the subtitle). Shrink pinned avatars to 64pt in a 4-up grid, matching Messages' pinned row.

24. **02-iphone-intelligence, 13 sidebar:** action rows are styled inconsistently. "Teams" is indigo with an outline icon inside the agents list; "New Agent" in the iPad sidebar is black with a black "+"; the iPhone puts "+" in the toolbar instead. **Fix:** move "Teams" to a section footer link or the toolbar menu, keep only agents in the agents list, and style every "add" row the same way (accent tint, `plus.circle.fill`).

25. **01, 02, 03, 04 toolbars:** a settings gear sits on every tab, plus a compose button, a "+", a refresh and a "…" that differ per tab. Settings isn't a per-tab action. **Fix:** remove the gear from the tabs and put a profile/workspace avatar button in the Dashboard's top-left (App Store / Photos pattern). The iPad sidebar header already has the right home for it.

26. **12-iphone-contact:** on a read view, "Add Email…" and "Add Phone…" rows sit in the same card as the data, and their separators start at the text (131px) while the Email/Phone separators start at the card edge (62px). The four action buttons are bare indigo glyphs, not the Contacts-style tiles. **Fix:** hide the add rows until Edit. Use one separator inset per card. Put the quick actions in 4 equal `.glass` rounded tiles (glyph above `.caption` label), as in iOS Contacts.

27. **06-iphone-actor-session, 13d:** the leading glass circle holding a spinner looks like a button but is only a status. The tool rows ("Edit Connection/Changes.swift ✓ 2s") are `.footnote` tertiary grey, too faint to scan. The code block is cut off at the right edge with no scroll affordance. In 13d the diff card and the permission card are narrower (~520pt) than the message column (~740pt). **Fix:** move "Working" status into the navigation subtitle ("apple · Working"). Set tool rows in `.subheadline` with the verb in `.secondary` semibold and the path in `.primary` monospaced. Fade the trailing edge of horizontally scrolling code. Make every card the full column width.

28. **13b-ipad-agent-chat:** the choice card's unselected options are grey text on grey and the selected option's check is a pale indigo, so the whole card looks disabled. The "Noted: …" and "Context rebuilt …" footnotes are caption2 tertiary and hard to read. **Fix:** once a choice is made, show the chosen row in `.primary` with a solid accent `checkmark.circle.fill`, and the others in `.secondary` (not `.tertiary`). Raise the footnotes to `.footnote` `.secondary`.

29. **13d-ipad-actor-session, 13 sidebar:** the selected actor row is filled indigo, so its orange "Needs you" turns white or grey and the most important signal disappears exactly when you're looking at it. The heavy filled-indigo selection is also stronger than iPadOS 26's sidebar selection. **Fix:** use the standard sidebar selection (tinted glass at low opacity, primary text), which keeps the orange status visible on selected rows.

30. **13-ipad vs 01-iphone:** the greeting copy changes ("Back at it, Jackson?" vs "Good afternoon, Jackson"). The question mark reads as uncertain. **Fix:** use one deterministic greeting by time of day, with no rhetorical question.

31. **01/02/03 tab bar:** the Dashboard tab glyph (two stacked filled bars) doesn't read as "dashboard" and is heavier than the others, and the four tabs mix filled and outline (sparkles is a hybrid). **Fix:** use `square.grid.2x2`, `house` or `rectangle.on.rectangle` for Dashboard, `sparkles` for Intelligence, `folder` or `square.stack` for Workspace (not a second grid), and `dot.radiowaves.up.forward` for Feeds, all at the tab bar's default (filled) variant.

## What works

- **34-article:** the serif reader is the best screen in the set: New York headlines, a comfortable measure, quiet meta and a linked source. It gives the app personality without breaking native rules.
- **06-iphone-actor-session / 13d:** the permission card (orange hairline border, hand glyph, monospaced command, prominent Allow, glass Deny, overflow) is a clear, distinctive "needs you" pattern. Keep it and copy its discipline elsewhere.
- **13c-ipad-tasks:** the iPad task table, with aligned date, assignee and flag columns and red overdue dates with a matching red calendar glyph, is dense, scannable and right for a professional tool.
- **04-iphone-feeds / 02:** unread shown as an accent dot plus a semibold title (Mail-style) is native and consistent between feeds and agents.
- **14-settings, 17-agent-info:** proper inset-grouped settings, a destructive action isolated in its own card, glass close buttons. These read as stock iOS in a good way.

## To reach 9+

1. **Lock the semantic palette (color, consistency).** Orange only means "needs you", red only failed/overdue, green only connected, indigo only the accent and selection. Rework priority flags to one hue at different strengths and size chips to a neutral graded mark, and recolour "Not Fetched". This alone fixes the largest drop in Color and removes the rainbow from Tasks and the widgets.
2. **One chrome system (native, consistency).** Turn on scroll-edge effects everywhere chrome floats (13b, 13d, 05). Use large titles that collapse to centred inline titles on every pushed list, and remove the dead band under the toolbar. Make one glass composer component and use it for Dashboard (ideally as the tab bar's bottom accessory), chat and actor sessions.
3. **Rebuild the Dashboard's hierarchy (hierarchy, spacing).** A one-line title with the greeting as a subtitle; inset-grouped cards for Due and What's New with primary-colour headers; tighter fixed gaps (16/24pt). On iPad, columns whose content fills the height, with no disabled-looking "Working" row.
4. **Unify iconography.** One SF Symbols family: outline accent symbols in lists and the sidebar, and restrained tiles only in Settings. Replace the grey letter tiles in Extensions, the unclear Dashboard tab glyph and the cropped Extensions glyph, and make offline/working states explicit marks rather than greyed text.
5. **Redesign the widgets and the compact Live Activity (polish, density).** Fill every size: three or four task rows in small and medium, trailing dates in all sizes, two columns in extra-large. Make the Agents medium and large show who needs you with names, not repeated avatars, and make the count agree across sizes. Put the needs-you count in the compact leading slot instead of the logo.
