## What changed in round 13, and why

All at the maintainer's word:

- Extensions: the version sits just after the name, not alone at the
  trailing edge, where a wrapped description left it floating.
- Agent chat (and actor session, to match): a soft scroll edge, so the
  avatar and pill float over the feed as in Messages, with no bar or line.
- Tasks: colour back, as the web has it: the size as a chip in its colour,
  tags as quiet pills, the flag grey/accent/yellow-orange/red by priority.
- Dashboard: the composer no longer lifts to the middle of the page as a
  message grows, and the keyboard's Done button is gone; scrolling the
  page dismisses the keyboard interactively.
- iPad sidebar: Actor Usage is in the system's overflow menu, and Settings
  is titled and kept in the bar longest, so a narrow sidebar overflows it
  into that menu rather than dropping it.
