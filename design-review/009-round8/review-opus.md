## Problems

1. **52-widget-agents-large**: the header says "Ada, Milo, and Juno need you" with a count of 3, but Ada's row reads "Reading 12 of 40 sources" in grey, so she's working, not waiting. The three ~145pt avatars also repeat the list right below them and take a third of the widget. Fix: drop the hero avatar strip on large. Lead with an orange "2 need you" line, then rows of avatar, name and the request text in orange. Put working agents in a second "Working" group with secondary subtitles. Make the count match the rows.
2. **07, 13c, 54, 55, 56 (orange flags), 14 (orange Profile tile), 02, 13, 50–52 (orange agent discs)**: orange is meant to say "needs you", but here it also means high priority, a settings category and an agent identity. On 13c an orange flag sits beside a red overdue date and reads like an alert. Fix: keep orange for needs-you only. Priority becomes red filled flag (urgent), label-colour filled flag (high), secondary outline flag (medium), no flag (low). Re-tint the Profile tile grey or indigo. Remove orange from the agent colour palette, or at least avoid it in the preview data.
3. **05, 06, 13b, 13d**: content shows sharply through the glass header and the composer. "Book flights for Lisbon" and "2026-11-15" sit legibly under "Message Atlas". Body text keeps going below the composer on 05. "Raise the middle card less…" overlaps the Atlas pill on 13b, and diff lines sit behind the title on 13d. On 06 a hard hairline clips the "Write Tests/…" tool row in half. Fix: add a soft scroll-edge effect at top and bottom, mount the composer as a bottom safe-area bar so nothing scrolls beneath it, and remove the hairline under the header.
4. **07 vs 13c, 01 vs 02/03/04**: the same Inbox is an inset-grouped card on iPhone and a plain full-bleed list on iPad. The iPhone Dashboard is plain white while every other tab root is grouped grey. Fix: pick one style per content type. Tasks should be plain lists on both devices, like Reminders, and the Dashboard should use the same grouped background as the other tabs, with What's New as an inset card.
5. **14, 17 vs 03, 04, 10 vs 44**: three icon systems. Settings and the agent's info sheet use rainbow rounded-square tiles; Workspace, Feeds and Documents use indigo outline glyphs; Extensions uses grey gradient monograms. On 17 the tiles add green (Usage) and magenta (Runs), which carry no meaning here. Fix: keep coloured tiles in Settings only, where it's the system convention. Make 17's rows secondary-colour SF Symbols, like Workspace. On 44, use each service's real mark, or a category symbol on a neutral tile.
6. **13c-ipad-tasks**: a ~660pt column centred in a ~1150pt detail pane leaves a ~230pt dead gutter. Six metadata items are packed onto one caption line, and the list name ("Launch", "Inbox") floats unmarked, so it reads like a tag. Fix: on iPad, use a `Table` with sortable Title, Due, Assignee, Size, List and Priority columns, or at least let the list fill the pane. Give the list name a `folder` glyph so it's distinct from #tags.
7. **01, 02, 03, 04, 07, 08, 10, 44**: title placement changes from screen to screen. Inbox's title sits left-aligned beside the back button. Journal, Documents and Extensions are centred inline. Workspace has only a centred "Studio ⌄" menu and no title. Intelligence and Feeds use large titles. Fix: every tab root gets a large title (Workspace becomes a large "Studio" with the switcher chevron), and every pushed screen gets a centred inline title, Inbox included.
8. **26-task-detail**: Size shows "Set Size" in indigo while every other value is a grey picker. Tags has no value. The "JZ" avatar touches "You". Notes need a separate "Edit Notes" row. The grey glass check top-right looks disabled and could mean either "save" or "complete". Due Date is on but shows no date. Fix: Size shows "None" in grey with up/down chevrons, Tags shows "None" or the tag chips, and the avatar gets 6pt of spacing. Make notes editable inline in a TextEditor. Use a "Done" text button for dismiss/save and add a separate prominent "Complete" action. Show the date row as soon as its toggle is on.

## What works

- The permission card (06, 13d): an orange outline and hand, the command in mono, a short explanation, and a clear indigo Allow next to a glass Deny. It's the strongest "needs you" moment in the app.
- The article reader (34): New York serif headings and body at a comfortable measure give the app the personality the brief asks for.
- The What's New rows (01, 13): a status glyph, the title, and a coloured status word with a relative time tell you what needs you at a glance.
- The Live Activity (57, 58): dense, well aligned, and its count row uses colour semantically. It's close to shippable.
- The iPad chat and session columns (13b, 13d) cap the reading width sensibly, and the agent's colour dot plus name are a light, distinctive author marker.

## To reach 9+

1. **Make orange mean one thing.** Rework priority flags (red / label / outline / none), re-tint the Profile tile, and keep orange out of agent colours. Then give every needs-you surface (widget counts, Island compact, What's New) the same orange hand. This lifts Color and Hierarchy most.
2. **Fix scroll edges and bottom bars.** Add soft top and bottom scroll-edge effects in chat and actor sessions, mount the composer as a safe-area bar, and remove the hairline under the header. Nothing should read legibly behind glass. This lifts Native and Polish.
3. **One icon language and one list style per content type.** Use tiles only in Settings and secondary glyphs elsewhere, with real marks for extensions. Tasks are plain lists on iPhone and iPad, tab roots share a grouped background and large titles, and pushed screens get centred inline titles. Replace the sync-arrow "working" glyph and the ruler size glyph.
4. **Rebuild the widgets around their job.** Large Agents: the waiting agents with their requests, no hero strip, and a count that matches. Small and medium Agents: an orange count, plus the requests on medium. Tasks: tighter rows (3 on small, 4 on medium), trailing dates at every size, two columns on XL.
5. **Give each detail screen one main action and finished states.** On the task sheet: a prominent Complete, Done to dismiss, inline notes and consistent picker values. On iPad tasks: a `Table` that fills the pane. Working rows should look alive, answered questions should show the chosen option clearly, and no caption should be below `.footnote`.
