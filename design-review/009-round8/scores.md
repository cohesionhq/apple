## What changed in round 8, and why

- **Dashboard**: only the status word takes its colour ("Needs you",
  "Failed"); the time after it is grey.
- **Agents widget**: a row's "Needs you" is amber, as every "needs you" is;
  what a working agent is doing stays secondary.
- **Contact**: the name is set as Contacts sets it, large and bold.
- **Extensions**: an inline title, as Settings above it has.
- **Preview data**: the Inbox holds seven tasks rather than two, which every
  reviewer read as an empty screen.
