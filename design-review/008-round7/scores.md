## What changed in round 7, and why

- **Live Activity**: the island's ring leads with how many need the user,
  in amber, while any do; else how many are running. Every reviewer read the
  old count (everything still going) as the done count.
- **Code blocks** fade out at the trailing edge, so a long line reads as
  scrolling on rather than cut mid-word.
- **Documents**: rows have a `doc.text` glyph, as Workspace's other lists'
  rows do, and their times are capitalised ("Yesterday").
- **Extensions**: the version sits at the trailing edge, clear of the name,
  and a description takes up to two lines. (Its inline title, committed
  with this, is round 8's and shows in round 8's shots.)
