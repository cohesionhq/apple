## What works

- The tab bar’s glass pill, indigo selection, and red count, plus the grouped trailing toolbar capsules, are correct iOS 27 chrome. Keep that and keep content cards opaque.
- The permission card in `06-iphone-actor-session-light.png` and `13d-ipad-actor-session-light.png`: orange stroke, the command in the card, one accent Allow, a quiet Deny.
- The article scale in `34-article-light.png`: large title, gray byline, indigo link, bold section headings, and a real code block at the end.
- The iPad What’s New / Working column in `13-ipad-split-view-light.png`: one status symbol, one line of state, color used as meaning. That column is the product’s visual language.
- The lock-screen Live Activity in `57-live-activity-lockscreen-light.png`: three sessions, elapsed time, “Needs you” in orange, and a compact summary. The small tasks widget in `53-widget-tasks-small-light.png` has the same glanceable quality.

## To reach 9+

1. **One row language.** Ship a single activity row and a single task row, and use them on the dashboard, Intelligence, both task lists, the widgets, and the Live Activity. This is the consistency and hierarchy jump; the iPad column and the lock screen already show the target.
2. **Fix the chrome insets and ship one code block.** Bottom inset on every iPhone tab, composer pinned above the transcript, code always monospaced and scrolled, never clipped. That removes the broken screens a designer would not ship.
3. **Lock the three status colors.** Orange only for needs-you, red only for failed and overdue, green only for connected. Recolor decorative tiles, agent discs, feed status, and priority flags that currently borrow those hues.
4. **Rebuild the two home surfaces.** Phone dashboard: greeting, needs-you, due, composer pinned above the tab bar. Workspace: secondary symbols and real counts, indigo only on the selection. The iPad sidebar follows the same rows so phone and pad are one design at two widths.
5. **One symbol family and one avatar.** SF Symbols at one weight in content, the app mark or grid for Dashboard, the in-app disc (glyph or initial, half the diameter, clipped) on every agent surface including the widgets. Retire the overlapping picture avatars and the gray extension letters.
