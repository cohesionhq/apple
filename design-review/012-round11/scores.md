## What changed in round 11, and why

At the maintainer's go-ahead on the four larger changes the reviewers kept
asking for:

- **Dashboard composer**: one glass bar, the agent menu inside it at the
  leading end with attach and send at the trailing end; Quick Journal is a
  `square.and.pencil` toolbar button rather than a second pill.
- **Due**: the web's Dashboard section, missing on iOS: open tasks due today
  or earlier, each with its checkbox, "Overdue" in red or "Today".
- **iPad Dashboard**: on a pane 900pt or wider, What's New and Working are a
  column beside the greeting, composer and Due.
- **One status vocabulary** in the app, widgets and Live Activity: working a
  dotted circle in the brand's colour, needs-you the circled hand in amber,
  done a grey check (no longer green in the Live Activity), failed a red
  filled cross.
- Not changed: the iPad sidebar's selection is iPadOS's own (solid while the
  sidebar has focus, tinted once the detail does).
- Tried and reverted at the maintainer's word: centring pushed titles
  (`toolbarTitleAlignment`, iOS 27.2); Inbox keeps its leading title, and the
  deployment target stays 27.0.

13c in this round is round 10's shot, taken before the flag column kept its
width, so its columns still drift there.
