## Problems

1. **52-widget-agents-large-light**: the header says "Ada, Milo, and Juno need you." but Ada's row says "Reading 12 of 40 sources" in gray, so she's working, not waiting on you. The widget contradicts itself. Count only agents whose state is needs-you in the headline (here "2 need you: Milo and Juno"). Take the oversized avatar cluster out of the large size entirely and lead with the list.
2. **13c-ipad-tasks-light**: the trailing columns aren't fixed-width. "Nov 11" (Read the Liquid Glass design notes) sits about 30pt right of every other date, and "Relay" on Triage open GitHub issues and Try the new Xcode build cache sits right of the "You" column. Give the date, assignee and flag columns fixed widths (or use a `Grid` / `Table` with alignment), leading-aligned, and leave an empty cell when a value is missing.
3. **13c-ipad-tasks-light**: no sidebar row is selected while All tasks is showing ("Tasks" isn't highlighted), unlike 13, 13b and 13d. Select "Tasks" whenever any task list is in the detail pane. The centered title "All ⌄" is also too weak for a primary pane: title it "All Tasks" and use the same title treatment as the other panes.
4. **05-iphone-agent-chat-light, 13b-ipad-agent-chat-light, 13d-ipad-actor-session-light**: transcript text runs visibly under the floating composer ("Record demo video / 2026-11-12" collides with "Message Atlas") and under the header ("Raise the middle card less" behind the Atlas capsule, diff lines behind the session title). Apply the system scroll-edge effect (`.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: [.top, .bottom])`), or a safe-area bar inset, so text fades under the glass instead of overlapping it.
5. **06-iphone-actor-session-light, 13b-ipad-agent-chat-light**: there are render artifacts. 06 has a blurred gray smear across the status bar above the Dynamic Island, and 13b has a cut-off blue blob in the top-right corner. Remove whatever stray glass or blur layer sits at the top edge.
6. **01-iphone-dashboard-light**: there are two competing "new" actions. The glass compose button in the toolbar duplicates the composer card right below it. Keep the composer as the screen's main action and swap the toolbar compose for nothing or for a profile/avatar button. On 13 the lone compose button in the top-right corner is especially orphaned.
7. **01-iphone-dashboard-light**: Dashboard is the only tab with a white, plain list, while Intelligence, Workspace and Feeds use gray, inset-grouped lists. Either make Due and What's New inset-grouped cards on `systemGroupedBackground` like the other tabs, or commit to plain lists everywhere. The mix reads as two apps.
8. **01-iphone-dashboard-light, 13-ipad-split-view-light**: section headers come in three styles. "Due 4" / "What's New 4" are about 22pt bold secondary, list headers ("Agents", "Tasks") are about 17pt semibold secondary, and sidebar headers are about 17pt regular secondary. Pick one, for example `.title3.weight(.semibold)` in the primary label colour with the count in `.secondary`, and use the system list-header style inside lists. The gray Dashboard headers in particular look disabled.
9. **13-ipad-split-view-light**: the two columns don't share a top line. "What's New" starts level with the greeting while "Due" starts about 260pt lower, under the composer. Put the greeting and the composer across both columns and start Due and What's New on the same baseline. The bottom half of the left column is also empty, so cap the overall width or balance the content.
10. **01, 13**: the Done status in What's New is an outlined check-in-circle, nearly the same glyph as the tappable task checkbox in Due right beside it. Use a filled green or gray `checkmark.circle.fill` for statuses so it can't be mistaken for a control. Also, "Working" rows render the title in tertiary gray, so they look disabled; keep the title primary and let the indigo "Working" label carry the state.
11. **04-iphone-feeds-light**: "Not Fetched" is orange, but orange means "needs you". It's also Title Case, while every other status ("Needs you", "Done") is sentence case. Use secondary gray "Not fetched", or red if it's a failure. Separate author and feed with " · " like everywhere else (it's currently a double space), and drop the chevrons on article rows: the unread dot and bold title are enough.
12. **50/51/52 widgets**: the avatar cluster dominates every size. On the medium, the right half is empty and "3" repeats the names next to it. Medium should be the cluster small (about 32pt) plus two or three rows of "Name: state". Small is fine but needs the agents' names or the app mark. Large: see item 1.
13. **53, 54, 56 tasks widgets**: there's a lot of dead space. Small shows two rows with a truncated "Review the ro…" and empty space below; medium shows two rows and leaves half its height blank; extra-large is one long list with about 800pt between title and date. Fit 3 rows in the small (tighter 22pt rows, smaller checkbox), 4 in the medium, and two columns or Overdue/Today/Tomorrow groups in the extra-large. Pick one date placement too: medium and extra-large put it trailing, large puts it under the title.
14. **01, 13c, 54–56**: overdue is shown three ways: "Overdue" (Dashboard), a red "Yesterday" / "Nov 7" with a red calendar (iPad list), and a red "Nov 3" (widgets). Pick one, for example a red relative date ("2 days ago" or "Overdue"), and use it on the list, Dashboard and widgets alike.
15. **26-task-detail-light, 07, 13c**: the monogram for the user is "JZ" in the task sheet but "J" in Inbox and All tasks. Use one initials rule. In the sheet the JZ disc also touches "You" with no gap; add 6pt.
16. **26-task-detail-light**: the controls are inconsistent and ambiguous. "Set Size" is an indigo button while every sibling is a gray menu value with ⌃⌄. Show "None ⌃⌄" like the others. The toolbar check is a gray-filled glass circle that reads as disabled and is ambiguous: it could be "save" or "complete", and complete duplicates the Status picker. Make it either an accent "Done" or a "Complete" action, not both. "Edit Notes ›" under the notes is clunky; make the notes text itself the editor (an inline `TextField(axis: .vertical)`).
17. **14, 17, 03, 02**: icon languages and meanings collide. The chat's Cue banner uses a lightning bolt, but in 17 the bolt is Runs and Cues are an alarm clock. Use the alarm clock for the Cue banner. On the tab bar, Dashboard and Workspace are heavy filled glyphs while Feeds is a thin outline; use the `.fill` variants consistently (or all outline). Contacts in 03 is a filled `person.crop.circle` among outline glyphs; use the outline.
18. **44-extensions-light**: every extension gets an identical gray letter tile ("G", "L", "D", "I", "C"), which looks like missing images. Give each a tinted tile, hashed from the name like agent colors, or an SF Symbol where the kind is known (GitHub/Linear: `arrow.triangle.branch`, Registry: `shippingbox`). Move the version ("v4") under the description as `.caption` secondary, or into the detail screen.
19. **02-iphone-intelligence-light**: two pinned agents float left in a large empty band, and Scout's offline state is only a dimmed label. Make the pins a horizontally scrolling row with status rings (orange ring for needs you, indigo for working) and dim the whole avatar for offline. "Teams" as an indigo row inside the Agents card is a link pretending to be a list item; move it to the section header as a trailing "Teams" button. The sidebar on 13 has the same problem, where "New Agent" is black and "Teams" is indigo.
20. **05-iphone-agent-chat-light, 13b**: the meta lines ("Noted: …", "Context rebuilt because provider cache expired", "Checking this week's tasks ›") are about 12pt tertiary and close to illegible. Use `.footnote` secondary at minimum. The unselected options in the choice card (A and C) are grayed so far they look disabled; keep them secondary, not tertiary, and give the selected one an indigo tint fill, not only a pale check.
21. **06, 13d**: the composer row is three separate glass objects (a spinner in a circle, the field, a stop square). The spinner reads as a button. Put the activity indicator inside the field's leading edge or in the header subtitle ("apple · working"), and make Stop the only trailing button. On 13d the permission card and the diff card are about 520pt wide while the code block and text run about 740pt; use one content width.
22. **10-iphone-documents-light**: the timestamps are body size and compete with the titles ("Yesterday" is as large as "Design Review — Nov 8"). Use `.subheadline` secondary. Repeating the same indigo doc icon on every row adds nothing; use a smaller secondary icon, or none.
23. **12-iphone-contact-light**: separator insets differ. Under Phone it starts at the text edge, under "Add Email…" it starts at the label after the plus. Align them all to the text. The four quick actions are bare tinted glyphs; iOS 26 Contacts uses glass tiles, so put each in a `.glassEffect()` rounded rectangle for a native, tappable look.
24. **07-iphone-tasks-light**: the title "Inbox" is small and leading beside the back button, while Journal and Documents are centered inline titles. Use the same title style for pushed lists (large title, or a centered inline title). Low-priority gray flags add noise to most rows; show a flag only for high and urgent.
25. **01, 02, 03, 04**: the Intelligence tab badge is system red for an item that needs you, which in this palette means failed. Since orange is "needs you", either badge with the orange tint (`.badgeProminence` with a tint) or accept red but never use orange for the same count elsewhere.
26. **57-live-activity-lockscreen-light**: "+3 more" is ambiguous (three more of what?) and the timers are unlabeled. Write "+3 done" and make the times clearly elapsed time ("0:40 elapsed", or a stopwatch glyph). The dotted working ring is hairline-thin at this size; thicken its stroke.

## What works

- The serif article reader (34): New York at a comfortable size and line length, hanging bullets and a clear byline. It's the best screen and gives the app a voice.
- The Live Activity (57–59): the counts strip, rows and segmented done/working/needs-you bar is information-dense, legible and on-palette, and the compact ring is a strong glanceable mark.
- The permission card (06/13d): an orange outline, a plain question, the command in mono, then Allow, Deny and More. It's the clearest "needs you" moment in the app.
- The colour semantics are mostly held: orange is needs you, red is failed or overdue, indigo is working, selection and links, and agents get their own colors.
- The chat header (avatar plus "Atlas ›" capsule) and the 740pt reading column on iPad make the chat feel native and calm.

## To reach 9+

1. **Redo the widgets around information, not avatars.** Agents: the headline counts only needs-you agents, small is a cluster plus names, medium and large are rows of name, state and time. Tasks: fill the small and medium (3 and 4 rows), group or split the extra-large, and use one date placement and one overdue style everywhere.
2. **Fix the iPad detail panes' structure.** Use fixed, aligned columns in All tasks, keep the sidebar selection on every pane, top-align the Dashboard's two columns under a shared greeting and composer, and use one content width in sessions.
3. **Use a single header and section system.** One section-header style across Dashboard, lists and the sidebar; one title treatment for pushed screens; Dashboard on the same grouped surface as the other tabs; secondary/subheadline metadata everywhere (Documents, Feeds).
4. **Clean up the icons.** One fill weight in the tab bar, filled discs for every status (never an outline that looks like a checkbox), one glyph per concept (Cue = alarm clock), and real or tinted tiles for Extensions.
5. **Finish the edges.** Add scroll-edge effects under every floating header and composer, remove the top-edge artifacts, unify separator insets and the "You" monogram, and give task-sheet controls one style with one unambiguous primary action.
