## What changed in round 12, and why

- **iPad task columns** line up (the round-11 shot predated the fix).
- **Done** is a filled grey check, so it never reads as a task's checkbox
  beside it.
- **Cue banner** in a chat is the alarm, as Cues is on the agent's sheet.
- **Documents' times** are `.subheadline`.

After the shots, at the maintainer's word, the Agents widget lists only the
agents that need the user and nothing about them past their avatars: no
status line (a widget's refresh is too slow to say what one is doing), no
rows beside the avatars at the large size, and its words in secondary grey
rather than amber. Every size is the same layout: overlapping avatars over
the count and who they are. The reviews here saw the earlier widgets.

## Open
