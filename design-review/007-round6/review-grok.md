## Problems

1. **02, 03, 04, 07, 08, 10, 14 — the product turns into Settings after the home screen.** Intelligence, Workspace, Feeds, Inbox, Journal, Documents, and Settings are the same inset grouped list: equal rows, gray section header, chevron. Workspace (`03`) is three identical menus with nothing that needs you. Fix: keep that chrome for settings only. Make Workspace a home for work — overdue count, the actor that needs you, then lists with counts — and use the dashboard status row (icon, title, colored state) as the row style for anything with a state.

2. **02, 03, 12, 01 — lists draw through the floating tab bar.** On Intelligence the next actor row (“Bump the companion…”) is visible under the glass; on Workspace, “Launch this week”; on the contact, the notes card slides under the bar. Fix: add a bottom content inset equal to the tab bar height plus 16pt, so the last row sits fully above the bar and the scroll-edge effect fades what passes beneath it.

3. **13b, 13d — iPad transcripts collide with the top bar.** Atlas’s chat opens on a cut sentence (“look disabled.”) under the avatar pill. The actor title and the top code card are faded and clipped by the same bar. Fix: inset the scroll view below the toolbar’s safe area. Keep the title in the toolbar; the first line of the transcript starts clear of it.

4. **01, 02, 13, 14, 17, 50–52, 54–59 — orange, red, and green mean three things at once.** Ledger is orange, and so is “Needs you,” and so are medium flags. Juniper is red, and so are failure, overdue dates, high-priority flags, and the Notifications icon. Scout is green, and the compact Live Activity’s “3” is a green ring, which reads as healthy while a session needs you. The agents widget then renders “Needs you” in gray. Fix: status color appears only on the status icon and its label. Move Ledger, Juniper, and Scout off orange, red, and green. Settings symbols use one neutral or indigo glyph, matching Workspace (`03`). Widget and compact Island status use the same orange hand as `01` and `06`.

5. **13c, 56 — the 13-inch task screen is a phone list stretched sideways.** `13c` is one full-bleed column of equal rows. Each secondary line is a caption string of date, person, size, tags, and list (“Nov 18 · Atlas · Huge · #q4 · Product”). On `56` the same rows sit on the left and the dates sit on the far right with a wide empty gap. Fix: landscape iPad is a split view, list plus the task sheet (`26`). In the list, show the title in body and one `.subheadline` secondary line with due date and assignee only. Align the flag in a trailing column. Build the extra-large widget as two columns of those rows, with the date immediately after the title.

6. **05, 06 — code and the table are cut off, so the work is illegible.** The chat table loses “2026-11-12” and the last rows under the composer. ## What works

- The dashboard status row on `01` and `13`: a leading status mark, a two-line title, and a colored “Needs you · 2m ago” / “Done · 18m ago” / “Failed · 1h ago”. That is the app’s visual language; the other screens should reuse it.
- The permission card on `06` and `13d`. One decision, an orange stroke, a filled Allow, a quiet Deny, a note field, and a separate stop button. It already feels designed.
- The article on `34`. Large title, byline, indigo source, a readable measure, and bold lead-ins in the bullets. This is the type standard for chat and the actor transcript.
- The iPhone tab bar and the circular glass toolbar buttons. Selected tab, accent label, and red badge are native Liquid Glass and stay consistent across the four tabs.
- Unpair (`14`) and Delete Agent (`17`) as isolated full-width red buttons, separate from the navigation groups. Same destructive pattern in both places.

## To reach 9+

1. **Lock the color contract and apply it everywhere.** Orange is only “needs you”, red is only failed, overdue, or destructive, green is only connected or succeeded. Recolor the agents that currently occupy those hues, flatten settings and agent-info icons to one indigo symbol style, and use the orange hand label on the widgets and the Live Activity. This is the change that moves Color and Consistency.
2. **Give iPad a column, not a stretched phone.** Cap chat, the actor transcript, and long reading text at about 640pt. Make tasks a split view with aligned title / due / assignee / flag, and rebuild the extra-large widget on that grid. This moves Typography, Hierarchy, Spacing, and Overall together.
3. **Stop drawing content under the glass.** Inset every scrolling screen above the floating tab bar and below the iPad toolbar, and use one header pattern: greeting on the home screen, large title plus toolbar menus on the other tabs, the same words on both devices. This moves Native and Consistency.
4. **One avatar component and one date formatter.** The disc used for Atlas (symbol or initial, agent color, fixed proportion) is the disc in the sidebar, the chat header, the contact, and widgets `50–52`. Dates follow a single relative-then-calendar rule, always `.subheadline` secondary. This removes the “two products” feeling between the app and the widgets.
5. **Replace equal rows with one primary fact.** Workspace leads with what needs you and a count, not three link menus. A task row carries a title and one secondary fact; size, tags, and list name stay in the sheet. Feeds show the latest item, not only a count. Short screens then have a real composition instead of a card floating on an empty page. This is what makes a professional believe the lists were designed.
