## Problems

1. **`02-iphone-intelligence-light.png`, `03-iphone-workspace-light.png`, `05-iphone-agent-chat-light.png`, `12-iphone-contact-light.png`, `01-iphone-dashboard-light.png`.** Rows and transcript text render through the liquid-glass tab bar and the chat composer. Intelligence shows “Bump the companion…” under the bar, Workspace shows “Cancelled” and “Launch this week” through the selected tab, the chat table and “Want me to draft…” sit under the composer, and the contact sheet ghosts type through the bar. A purple sliver also sticks out at the lower left of the dashboard tab bar. Give every scrolling screen a bottom inset equal to the bar plus 12pt, and mask the content so nothing draws inside the glass. Make the chat composer a real bottom bar that reserves its height.

2. **`06-iphone-actor-session-light.png`.** The screen that asks for permission cannot show the permission’s subject or its code. The title is cut to “Add backoff to the change …”, and the code block clips mid-glyph (`Dura`, `atte`). Let the nav title wrap to two lines at `.headline` so the full task name is visible. Put code in a horizontal scroll view, monospaced at 13pt, with 16pt of internal padding, and truncate file paths in the middle (`Tests/…/BackoffTests.swift`) at `.footnote`.

3. **`13-ipad-split-view-light.png`, `13b-ipad-agent-chat-light.png`, `13c-ipad-tasks-light.png`, `13d-ipad-actor-session-light.png`.** The sidebar’s last row collides with the home indicator. 4. **`13b-ipad-agent-chat-light.png`, `05-iphone-agent-chat-light.png`.** The chat header is a stacked avatar and a small “Atlas ›” pill, and on iPad the transcript scrolls underneath it (“look disabled.” is faded behind the avatar). ## What works

- The status trio is immediate and repeatable: orange hand and “Needs you”, gray check and “Done”, red cross and “Failed”, on the dashboard, the actor row, and the permission card.
- The iOS 27 chrome is the right vocabulary where it is not overlapping content: floating tab bar, capsule `+` / filter / `…` groups, circular sheet close, and an iPad sidebar with a clear selected pill.
- The permission card has one job. The command sits in a well, Allow is the only filled accent button, Deny is secondary, and more is a separate circle.
- The article earns its type: a real title, a byline and source, a comfortable measure, inline code chips, and headings that break the page.
- Agent color plus a symbol or initial makes Atlas, Scout, Quill, and the sidebar scannable, and Quill’s unread dot is the right quiet signal.

## To reach 9+

1. **Make the glass hold still.** One content-inset and mask rule for the tab bar, the chat composer, the iPad header, and the sidebar’s bottom edge. This is the single change that most affects Native, Spacing, and Consistency, because the bug is on the screens people see first.
2. **Install one type and date scale.** `.body` for the thing, `.subheadline` secondary for dates and metadata, `.footnote` only for paths and versions. Nothing you must read at `.caption2`. One date formatter. Code scrolls, it does not clip. That lifts Typography and the unfinished feel of every list.
3. **Use one grid.** Inset grouped lists on iPhone, a measured column (about 640–680pt) for dashboard, chat, and actor sessions on iPad, and widgets that add rows or a second column as they grow. The same task row on the phone, the iPad, and the widget.
4. **Lock the palette.** Indigo is accent and navigation. Orange is needs you. Red is failed or overdue. Green is running or connected. Settings and info lose the rainbow tiles. Agent hues move off orange and red. Status strings use the status color on every surface, including the small widget.
5. **Redesign the two work screens.** The dashboard leads with what needs you, with the same structure on both devices, greeting in `.title2`. The task list is grouped Overdue / Today / Upcoming, with a readable meta line and one flag. Those two changes move Hierarchy and Overall more than any polish pass on the directory screens.
